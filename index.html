Leather Care & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

We recommend storing your handbag in its complimentary organic cotton dust bag when not in use, stuffed lightly with acid-free tissue paper to preserve its shape. Apply a neutral beeswax or organic leather cream once every 6 months, avoiding excessive contact with direct rain or alcohol sanitizers.
Our smooth calfskin and pebbled leathers feature a micro-thin botanical sealant that repels light raindrops. If caught in a downpour, gently blot (do not rub) with a clean microfiber towel and allow it to dry naturally at room temperature away from radiators.
All orders are processed from our Los Angeles fulfillment atelier within 24–48 hours. Continental US standard delivery arrives in 3–5 business days, and complimentary expedited 2-day delivery is granted on purchases over $150 USD.
We welcome returns and size exchanges within 30 days of carrier delivery. Handbags must remain in unworn, brand-new condition with security authenticity tags intact and housed in the original protective box and dust sleeve.
